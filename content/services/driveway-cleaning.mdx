## What builds up on a Florida driveway?

Concrete and paver driveways here pick up tire marks, oil, rust from sprinklers and well water, mildew and algae. Surface cleaning removes the film and most of the marks.

## Will the rust and oil stains come out?

Rust often improves, but not always fully. Some rust and oil stains need spot treatment and may not come out completely.

## How long does it take?

A typical driveway takes a few hours, depending on its size and how much buildup is on it.
