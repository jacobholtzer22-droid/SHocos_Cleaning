## Why does my house turn green?

Florida humidity, salt air and shade grow algae, mildew and mold on exterior walls. It shows first on shaded walls and under the eaves, as green and black streaks over a general dirt film.

## Will a house wash damage paint or stucco?

Most exterior surfaces are cleaned with controlled pressure matched to the surface, which is what lets a wash take off the growth without harming the finish underneath.

## Should I wash before painting?

Typically, yes. A clean exterior is what paint and sealers need before they go on, so a house wash is a common first step before a paint job.
