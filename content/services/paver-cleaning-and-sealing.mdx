{config.displayName} cleans and seals pavers for homes on the Treasure Coast and in Palm Beach County, and maintains pavers that were sealed before. Paver cleaning and sealing is one of the two specialties.

## What does paver cleaning take out?

Pavers collect dirt, algae and weeds in the joints. Cleaning lifts that out of the joints and off the surface, and joint sand is typically replaced after cleaning.

## What does sealing do?

A sealer typically deepens the color, locks the joint sand in place, and slows new growth and staining. The before and after photos on this page show the difference on two driveways.

## How often do pavers need resealing?

Typically every few years, depending on sun, traffic and sprinklers. Paver maintenance means periodic cleaning and resealing of pavers that were sealed before.
