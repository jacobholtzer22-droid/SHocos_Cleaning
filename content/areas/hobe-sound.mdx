## What do Hobe Sound homes usually need?

Hobe Sound is an unincorporated community in southern Martin County, on the mainland across the water from Jupiter Island. Coastal humidity and salt air grow algae and mildew on house walls, pavers and pool decks here like everywhere else on the Treasure Coast.
