## What do Sebastian homes usually need?

Sebastian sits on the Indian River Lagoon in northern Indian River County, south of the Sebastian Inlet. Humidity and the lagoon air grow algae and mildew on house walls, driveways and pavers. A house wash and a driveway cleaning take it off.
