## What do Stuart homes usually need?

Stuart is the county seat of Martin County, on the St. Lucie River near where it meets the Indian River Lagoon. Waterfront homes along the river get salt air and humidity on stucco, screens and pool decks, and the algae and mildew that come with it.

## Do you clean pool decks and enclosures here?

Yes. Screened pools are common in Stuart, and the decks and cages are part of the same cleaning list as the house and the driveway.
