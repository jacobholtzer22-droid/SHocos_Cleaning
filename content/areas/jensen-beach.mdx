## What do Jensen Beach homes usually need?

Jensen Beach is an unincorporated community in Martin County, north of Stuart along the Indian River Lagoon, with part of it on Hutchinson Island. Homes near the lagoon and the ocean get salt air and humidity, which grows algae and mildew on stucco, screens and pavers.
