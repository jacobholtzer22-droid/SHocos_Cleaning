## What do Palm Beach Gardens homes usually need?

Palm Beach Gardens is in northern Palm Beach County and is known for its golf communities, including PGA National. Many neighborhoods are gated or run by homeowners associations, where paver driveways, screened pools and stucco exteriors are kept to a standard, and cleaning and resealing are part of keeping up with it.
