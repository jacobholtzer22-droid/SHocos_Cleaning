## What do Palm City homes usually need?

Palm City is an unincorporated community in Martin County, across the St. Lucie River from Stuart. Much of it is residential neighborhoods, many with homeowners associations, where paver driveways and screened pools are common and clean, sealed pavers are part of keeping a home looking kept up.
