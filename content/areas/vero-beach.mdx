## What do Vero Beach homes usually need?

Vero Beach is the county seat of Indian River County, with a mainland side and a beachside on the barrier island across the Indian River Lagoon. Beachside homes get the most salt air, and the city's established neighborhoods have older concrete driveways and walks that darken with mildew over time.
