## What do Fort Pierce homes usually need?

Fort Pierce is the county seat of St. Lucie County, on the Indian River Lagoon with an inlet to the ocean. Homes near the water get salt air on every surface, and the city's older neighborhoods have plenty of established concrete driveways that darken with mildew and tire marks over the years.

## What about homes on Hutchinson Island?

North Hutchinson Island sits across the lagoon from the city. Oceanfront and lagoon-side homes there get the most salt air, which is why stucco and screens near the water tend to need cleaning more often.
