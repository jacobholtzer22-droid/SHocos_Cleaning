## What do Port St. Lucie homes usually need?

Port St. Lucie is the largest city in St. Lucie County, and most of it is residential. Much of it was built out in newer subdivisions, including the master-planned communities of St. Lucie West and Tradition on the west side, where paver driveways and screened pools are common. Those pavers fade and fill with weeds, and the pool cages collect mildew.

## Do HOA communities need anything different?

Many neighborhoods here have homeowners associations with standards for how the outside of a home looks. A house wash and clean, sealed pavers are the usual way to keep up with them.
