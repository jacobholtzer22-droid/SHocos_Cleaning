## What do Jupiter homes usually need?

Jupiter is in northern Palm Beach County, around the Jupiter Inlet and the Loxahatchee River. Waterfront homes get salt air on stucco, screens and pool decks, and the many homeowners association communities here expect clean walls and clean, sealed pavers.
