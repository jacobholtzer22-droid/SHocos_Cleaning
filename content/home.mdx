## What does pressure cleaning do for a Florida home?

Humidity, salt air and shade grow algae, mildew and mold on almost every outdoor surface here. Stucco goes green and black in streaks, pavers fade and fill with weeds, concrete driveways darken, and pool decks get slick. Pressure cleaning takes that off, with the pressure matched to each surface.

## What are the specialties?

House pressure washing, and paver cleaning and sealing. Driveways, pool decks, pool enclosures and gutters round out the list, so the whole outside of the house can be done by one company.
